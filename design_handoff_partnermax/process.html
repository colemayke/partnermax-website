02 / Five steps
01.
Step 01 · Inquire

Tell us what you need.

One form, an email, or a phone call. We just need to know the service shape — wireless lines, fibre circuits, voice services, or all of it — and the rough scale.

  • Submit the form on the homepage, or email hello@partnermax.ca.
  • Tell us service type, fleet or site size, and provinces of operation.
  • If you have existing Rogers accounts, send the account numbers.
  • Reseller? Tell us — that path has a separate qualification.
Timing Same business day

A named channel manager replies before close of business with next steps.

What you receive A direct reply

Not a ticket number. The person who replies is the person you’ll work with end-to-end.

What we need from you Five minutes

A short form is enough. We’ll fill the gaps on the call.

02.
Step 02 · Free consultation

Thirty minutes to size the deal.

A working session, not a pitch. Your channel manager walks through the scope, the geography, the existing contracts you’re replacing, and the constraints we should know about.

  • Video or in-person at our Front Street office — your call.
  • We map your service needs onto the Rogers product set.
  • We flag anything Rogers can’t cover (so you can plan around it).
  • We agree on the shape of the quote before we go build it.
Timing Day 2–3

Booked the same day as your inquiry. 30 minutes is the standard slot.

Cost $0

No engagement fee. No commitment. You walk away with a clearer plan either way.

Who attends Your two leads

Your channel manager and (if relevant) the provisioning lead who’ll handle activation.

03.
Step 03 · Quote

A written, itemized
proposal.

One PDF. Every line item visible. The price you sign at is the price on the first invoice — we don’t add provisioning fees, channel surcharges, or activation costs after the fact.

  • Itemized by service, line, site and term length.
  • Includes Rogers-side SLAs and the PartnerMax operations SLA.
  • Includes the cancellation terms and the renewal terms.
  • Valid for 30 days; locks the pricing for the contract term.
Timing Day 4–7

Three to four business days after consultation for a standard quote.

Validity 30 days

The quote holds for a calendar month. Hardware availability can shift it earlier — we’ll flag if it does.

Revisions Unlimited

Adjust the scope as many times as you need before signing. No charge for revisions.

04.
Step 04 · Provision

We turn it on.

The operational heart of PartnerMax. Your named provisioning lead coordinates Rogers, sequences hardware delivery, and activates every line and circuit on the agreed cutover date.

  • SIMs, devices and routers shipped direct to each site.
  • Number porting handled inside Rogers, no downtime windows.
  • Fibre installs scheduled with the carrier’s field team.
  • Acceptance testing on every line before we mark complete.
Timing Day 8–18

Wireless: 3–5 days. Fibre: 10–14 days. Voice porting: 7–10 days. Run in parallel.

Hand-off A single
cutover date

You pick the calendar day. Everything lights up on that day, not in pieces.

Support coverage 24/7

From cutover onward. Provisioning lead stays on the account through the first invoice cycle.

05.
Step 05 · Bill

One invoice, monthly.

Wireless, fibre, voice, hardware financing — consolidated. Itemized by line and site. Net-30 terms. Edit anytime through your channel manager or your partner console.

  • One PDF invoice per month, plus machine-readable CSV.
  • Line-level detail, with monthly cost and usage variance.
  • Renewal notices 90 days out — no surprise rollovers.
  • Account changes processed inside one business day.
First invoice Day 21+

Issued the calendar month after cutover. Pro-rated for partial months.

Terms Net-30

Wire, EFT or pre-authorized debit. We’ll accommodate your AP workflow.

Renewal 90 day notice

Always. Every contract. You choose every cycle whether to keep the relationship.
