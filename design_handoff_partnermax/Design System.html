01Logotype p. 002

The PartnerMax wordmark.

Our identity is a single confident wordmark. “PARTNER” reads in neutral weight, “MAX” carries the brand red and the custom M — a sharp rooftop angle that signals scale and the peak of partnership.

Primary lockup

The dark hero is the canonical context. The wordmark should breathe — never crowd it with photography or competing type.

Variants

Use the variant that gives the cleanest contrast for the surface.

01 · Primary on dark
02 · Primary on light
03 · Reverse on red
04 · Mono dark / editorial
05 · Mono white / utility
06 · One-color red

Clear space

Reserve clear space equal to the height of the cap-height (the “P”) on all sides. No type, image or graphic element may enter this zone.

× × × ×

Minimum size

The wordmark must remain legible. Never render below the minimum size on its respective medium.

Digital · 96 px wide
Print · 24 mm wide

Misuse

Eight things never to do with the wordmark.

× Don’t rotate
The wordmark always sits horizontal.
× Don’t stretch
Preserve the original proportions.
× Don’t flip the variant
Use the light-on-dark version on dark surfaces.
× Don’t place on busy gradients
Use a flat brand surface or photo with safe zone.
.io
× Don’t add taglines inline
Keep the wordmark as a standalone unit.
× Don’t recolor the MAX
Red is fixed; do not substitute another color.
× Don’t add effects
No drop shadows, glow, bevel or 3D.
× Don’t enclose
The wordmark stands alone; no containers.

03Typography p. 004

Sharp display.
Considered prose.

Three typefaces, each with a clear role. Bai Jamjuree handles every moment that wants to assert — titles, navigation, buttons. Bai Jamjuree Italic handles every moment that wants to explain — body copy, decks, narrative. Manrope handles the UI and any place a screen needs to disappear.

Aa
01 · Display + headings Bai Jamjuree

Confident, modern grotesque. Use for every headline, every button, every navigation label. Tight tracking at display sizes.

Aa

Modern serif for long-form. Always paired with Bai Jamjuree — never used alone for a headline. The italic is our accent.

05Iconography p. 006

Engineered to a 24 grid.

08Voice & tone p. 009

Authoritative.
Engineered.
Plainspoken.

We are a B2B channel. We sell trust before we sell SKUs. Our writing reads like a partner who knows the network — specific, calm, and confident enough not to oversell.

Three principles

01

Specific, not generic.

Numbers, names, and concrete outcomes. “Provisioned in 14 days” beats “fast turnaround”.

02

Direct, not slick.

Write the way you’d brief an ops director: short sentences, no superlatives, no jargon for its own sake.

03

Partner-first.

Frame the product around what our channel partners do with it — not the technology spec sheet.

Voice in practice

Yes

“Pool 50 lines across your fleet. One bill, one partner, one renewal date.”

Not this

“Unlock seamless next-generation wireless solutions and supercharge your business communications.”

Yes

“We sell what Rogers builds — sized for businesses Rogers doesn’t reach directly.”

Not this

“PartnerMax is your trusted partner in the journey to digital transformation.”

Words we use · words we don’t

Use
  • Partner · Channel · License · Quote
  • Provision · Activate · Renew · Pool
  • Business · Operator · Fleet · Site
  • Direct · Honest · Specific · Engineered
Avoid
  • Synergy · Seamless · Best-in-class
  • Unlock · Empower · Supercharge
  • Solution (when product will do)
  • Journey · Transform · Disrupt

10Tokens · for Claude Code & Claude Design p. 011

Drop these into your project.

Every visual decision in this system is defined as a CSS custom property in tokens.css. Import it once and reference variables — never hardcode.

1 · Import

/* In your root stylesheet */
@import "./tokens.css";

/* Or via <link> */
<link rel="stylesheet" href="tokens.css" />

2 · Use the variables

.hero {
  background: var(--pm-ink-900);
  color: var(--pm-ink-0);
  padding: var(--pm-space-9);
  font-family: var(--pm-font-display);
}
.cta {
  background: var(--pm-red-600);
  border-radius: var(--pm-radius-sm);
}

3 · Switch themes per surface

// Anywhere in the DOM tree
<div data-theme="dark">  // premium / hero / deck
<div data-theme="ivory"> // editorial / long-form
<div>                  // default: light surface
